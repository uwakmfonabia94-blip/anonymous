Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Anonymous is a private, invite-only platform for university students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Email and password — to create and secure your account. Passwords are hashed; we never see them.
  • Your posts, replies, upvotes, and reports — the content you create on the platform.
  • An auto-generated alias (e.g. "Quiet Comet #482") — this is your public identity on the platform.
  • An IP address — logged by our infrastructure provider for security and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

  • Your real name, phone number, photos, or location.
  • Your browsing history outside of Anonymous.
  • Any data from your device beyond what a standard website request contains.

What other people can see

Everyone on the platform sees your posts and replies under your alias. Nobody — not other users, not admins through the normal product — sees your email attached to a post. Admin tools exist for moderation only and are protected by strong access controls.

Who we share with

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run Anonymous: Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting). They process your data on our behalf. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

How long we keep it

Your account and content are kept until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your email, profile, posts, replies, and upvotes are permanently deleted from our database.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated content at any time from Me → Delete account. If you have questions or need help, contact hello@anonymous.app.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we will notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
