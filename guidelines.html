Community Guidelines

Read these before you post. They exist to keep Anonymous safe, and breaking them will get you removed.

Anonymous is not a shield

Being anonymous does not mean you get to hurt people. The whole point of this space is to be honest without being cruel. If what you're about to say would hurt a real person you know, don't say it here.

Never do these

  • Threats of violence. Anything that suggests harming someone — even as a "joke" — gets you banned and may be reported to campus authorities.
  • Doxxing. Sharing someone's real name, address, phone number, social media, or any identifying detail, even partially, is prohibited.
  • Targeted harassment. Posting about a specific person with the goal of humiliating, degrading, or intimidating them.
  • Slurs and hate speech. Against any race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality.
  • Sexual content involving minors. Any hint of it, and we report to law enforcement.
  • Illegal content. Drugs, weapons, stolen goods, or anything prohibited by law.
  • Spam and impersonation. Fake accounts, bot-like posting, or pretending to be a staff member or another user.

What we encourage

Confessions, questions, campus gossip that's actually funny, real talk, hot takes, and anything that brings the campus together without taking someone down.

If you see something wrong

Tap the ⋯ on the post or comment and select Report. Every report is reviewed by a human (the founding team, at first).

Enforcement

We enforce these guidelines with a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban, depending on severity. For threats and doxxing, we skip straight to a permanent ban. In serious cases, we contact campus security.

Your real identity is our responsibility

We protect your anonymity from other users. But if you break these guidelines, we will use our internal admin tools to identify you and take action. That's the deal: be decent, and stay anonymous; be cruel, and we know who you are.
